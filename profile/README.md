Hiring should not come down to a number nobody can check. Candrecovia brings candidates, recruiters and companies onto one platform where every match is scored, every score is explained, and every search stays private.

Candidates see exactly where they stand and what to learn next. Recruiters shortlist with reasons they can stand behind. Companies hire fairly, with every decision on the record.

## 🧭 How a match works

<table>
<tr>
<td width="20%" valign="top">
<h3>1. Read</h3>
A CV and profile become a set of skills, each matched to a shared skills taxonomy.
</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top">
<h3>2. Describe</h3>
A role lists its requirements in the same taxonomy, each essential or optional.
</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top">
<h3>3. Compare</h3>
The matching engine sees skills only. Never a name, an email or an employer.
</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top">
<h3>4. Explain</h3>
The score arrives with its working: what was met, what was missing, and how much each counted.
</td>
<td width="20%" valign="top">
<h3>5. Improve</h3>
Missing skills become a ranked plan, weighted by impact on fit and by demand in the market.
</td>
</tr>
</table>
